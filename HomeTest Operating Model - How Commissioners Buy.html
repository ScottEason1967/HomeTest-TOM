The commercial model · demand side

How commissioners buy.

Commissioners buy from the framework, not from NHSE. They call off from the accredited register, at or below a national maximum price, and they pay the supplier directly.

Agreed in principle. Not final. Sufficient for models and SLT records; not sufficient for a tender.
Call-offs below a national maximum price.
NHSE sets a national maximum price for each test. A commissioner does its own call-off from the register and gets a lower price with competition and volume. NHSE does not guarantee volumes; it shares forecasts. The framework agreement holds the entry terms, the price fundamentals and the performance management. The call-off holds the order terms and the local terms. Accreditation is in the schedules.

Payment for each activity, not for each completed test.
Each stage of the cycle has a price: delivery, sample, analysis, result. If a cycle completes 70 percent of its stages, the supplier receives 70 percent of the payment. The completion rate is a programme measurement, not a payment condition, because the supplier does not control all causes of non-completion. KPIs in the contracts give the remedies. Service credits are not used.

Money flows directly.
The supplier invoices the commissioner at the rates and frequency in the call-off. The commissioner pays from its own budget. NHSE does not hold a budget and does not touch payments.
The money loop →
Volume tiers: open.
The plan is prices that decrease when volume increases, with a fairness mechanism: the first buyers must not pay more than later, larger buyers. There is tension between volume discounts and a market with no maximum number of suppliers. Both options are in the model. There is no decision.
Choice on the register.
The register shows each accredited supplier for each open category. A commissioner compares suppliers on price and on the differences above the minimum standard: device, speed, service. More suppliers in a category cause lower prices below the maximum.

An NHS organisation can also serve itself.
A commissioner or a pathology network can do its own home sampling instead of a purchase from the framework. NHSE gives help through a community of practice and a concierge service, off the framework.

Where it stands

Agreed in principle. Open items.

The vehicleAn open framework under the Procurement Act 2023, operated by a framework provider. In-house is kept in reserve.
The termThree years plus one, the work position. Reopening approximately each six months at the start.
The sizeApproximately £250m for the framework life, agreed in principle. Conservative, contingency included.
The dateGo-live in calendar Q1 2028; anchor 1 April 2028. August 2027 is not possible.
The disciplineSimple to operate and transparent. If many people are necessary to calculate an invoice, the design is wrong.
Agreed in principle
  • One master lot for the full journey is the preferred position, being stress-tested against a multi-lot approach; the supplementary clinical-support and professional-services lots are set aside for round one
  • A framework provider operates the framework; in-house kept in reserve
  • Consortiums permitted with one nominated prime; NHS bids permitted
  • Open to each qualified supplier; no maximum; minimum target approximately three for each test
  • Two assurance steps, gates approximately each six months; tests accredited, not use cases
  • A levy of one to two percent, with a part returned to NHSE as a platform fee on throughput
  • Payment for each activity; a national maximum price; KPIs give the remedies
  • Insourcers get help off the framework
Open items
  • Repatriation of funds: name, mechanism, VAT, competition risk. This blocks the return line
  • The connection between NHSE assurance and the provider process
  • The proof at tender: a service that operates, or one ready at service start
  • The content of the test standard; a route for laboratories that do not have it yet
  • Go-live before full platform integration
  • Paid innovation for roadmap items; publication of throughput data
  • The activities, the price book, the tender template
  • The tiers, the gain-share, the fairness mechanism
  • Reopening frequency; new prices for current suppliers at reopening
  • NHSE costs and break-even; interim cover to Q1 2028; host start dates
